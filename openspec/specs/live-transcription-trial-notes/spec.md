## Requirements
### Requirement: Preserve live transcription trial observations
The project SHALL include a Markdown document that records the initial local microphone transcription setup, chunk-duration observations, known limitations, and next experiments.

#### Scenario: Resume future experimentation
- **WHEN** a developer returns to the live transcription prototype
- **THEN** they SHALL be able to identify the current recommended chunk-duration range and unresolved reliability concerns from the trial-notes document
